## Common Questions

### Q: Can I use this for free?

**A:** Yes! Completely free forever for:
- Personal use
- Repairing your own computers
- Helping friends and family
- Educational purposes
- Internal company use

---

### Q: Can I use this in my IT company?

**A:** Yes, you can use the tool internally for repairing employee or client computers as part of your services. This is considered internal use.

However, you CANNOT:
- Sell the tool itself separately
- Create a paid service that just wraps this code
- Charge money specifically for "NewEra Revive as a service"

---

### Q: Can I modify the code and use it in my product?

**A:**
- If your product is free - yes
- If your product is commercial and competes with NewEra Revive - no
- If it's part of a larger product and not the main feature - maybe, contact us for clarification

---

### Q: Can I charge for installation/setup?

**A:** Yes, you can charge for your time, expertise, installation, setup, and consulting services. You're selling your labor, not the tool itself.

---

### Q: What counts as "commercial use"?

**A:** Commercial use includes:
- Selling the Software itself
- Offering paid access to the Software
- Creating a competing commercial product
- Using the Software as the primary value of a paid service

NOT commercial use:
- Using it as one of many tools in your toolkit
- Internal company use (even in for-profit companies)
- Free help to others
- Charging for YOUR time and expertise (not the tool)

---

### Q: Can I host it on my website for download?

**A:** Yes, if:
- Download is free
- You keep original license and attribution
- You don't modify code without noting changes
- You don't claim authorship

---

### Q: Will this ever become open for commercial use?

**A:** No. This license has no expiration or automatic conversion to a commercial-friendly license. The non-commercial restriction is permanent.

If you need commercial use rights, contact the maintainers for a separate commercial license.

---

### Q: How to get permission for commercial use?

**A:** Contact the project maintainers to discuss a separate commercial license. We can provide commercial licenses on mutually beneficial terms.
